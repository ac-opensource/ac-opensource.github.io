35mm Weekends: A Film Photography Gallery

Published September 14, 2024

I carry a Nikon FM2 in the tote bag that also holds my laptop. Between commits, I sneak out for walks to catalog the colors that rarely make it into UI mocks. Here are a few frames from recent rolls. All were home-developed in Cinestill chemistry and scanned with a DSLR rig I hacked together from spare parts.

I log exposure settings in Notion templates the same way I track experiments for mobile features. The structure keeps me honest about what worked and what was pure luck. When the scans arrive, I tag each frame with the lens, meter, and chemistry. It sounds obsessive, but it lets me remix the look quickly when a freelance design gig needs texture.

Want a physical print? Send me the frame label and I'll drop a signed 5x7 in the mail.
