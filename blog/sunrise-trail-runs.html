Sunrise Trail Runs & Flow States

Logged on November 2, 2024

The best sprint planning I do now starts on dirt. I set the alarm for 3:45 AM, grab cold brew from the fridge, and drive straight to the Marcos Highway jump-off while the city is still asleep. The first kilometer is always the loudest—knees snapping, headlamp bouncing—but the cadence smooths out once the second climb opens up to the Sierra Madre horizon.

Training block highlights:

  • Route: 14 km lollypop looping through KM90 aid station, 680 m elevation gain.
  • Fuel: 500 ml soft flask with plain water plus a second flask with cold brew + coconut water.
  • Tempo: Keep power hiking under 160 bpm so I can negative split the descents.

I keep a rotating playlist of mostly instrumental house for these runs. Anything with a clean 90–95 BPM beat keeps foot placement patient on the technical switchbacks. The last 20 minutes are headphones-off so I can hear birds cutting through the ravine and mentally rehearse the week ahead.
